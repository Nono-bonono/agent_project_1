# Ed Miliband on AI

## Ed Miliband on AI

Evidence is thin. Hansard has five short ministerial replies and no substantive AI speech. The web search found no article where Miliband sets out his own views on AI.

### Overall position
In Parliament, Miliband is supportive of AI and treats it mainly as an energy-policy issue and a growth opportunity. He also acknowledges security threats from it. The web sources give no personal statement from him. They only show him sitting on the government's AI Energy Council, where commentators see possible tension with his clean-power goals.

### Key arguments
All of these come from Hansard. The agent gave dates and debate titles but no URLs.
- **AI and energy demand (4 Feb 2025, Topical Questions):**
  - He said AI has "huge potential".
  - He would chair an AI energy council with the Science, Innovation and Technology Secretary.
  - The council would look at how to meet AI's future energy demand, and how AI can help deliver the future energy system.
- **Backing the government's AI agenda (29 Apr 2025, Topical Questions):** Responding to the shadow minister on the Tony Blair Institute report, he agreed with much of it. That included moving ahead on AI, which he said the government is doing.
- **AI growth zones (10 Jun 2025, Topical Questions):** He called the idea of AI growth zones "great". He also praised a colleague's related local initiative.
- **Defending the AI record (6 Jan 2026, Gas-fired Power Stations):** He said "we are in favour of AI and we are working with our colleagues on AI". He then used the exchange to attack a former Energy Secretary over the cost of renewable power.
- **Threats and opportunities (27 Oct 2026, Foreign Interference in Democratic Processes):**
  - He agreed on the need to counter "all the threats we face from AI, as well as taking advantage of the opportunities".
  - He said the new centre announced by the Prime Minister would do this.
  - Check this date. It may be a transcription error, or it may be later than the other items suggest.

### Shift over time
I found no real change of view. The emphasis broadens from AI's energy needs and growth in 2025 to AI-related security and interference threats later. That may reflect the debate topic rather than a change of position.

### Parliament vs media
- **Agreements:**
  - Both sources place Miliband's AI role in the energy context, through the AI Energy Council with the Science Secretary (now named as Liz Kendall).
  - The web sources do not contradict his pro-AI stance.
- **Contradictions:** None are directly evidenced.
  - PoliticsHome/The House reports "internal friction" between the science department (DSIT) and the energy department (DESNZ) over gas-powered data centres. The source is an unnamed expert, and it is not attributed to Miliband.

  - Polly Billington, a Labour MP and Miliband's former adviser, warned that gas-powered data centres endanger net-zero targets.
